Kamikaze Hero Privacy Policy

Kumamoto Games. Effective 25 November 2026.

This policy covers the game Kamikaze Hero on Android (Google Play) and on Windows (Steam).

Short version

Kamikaze Hero does not collect, store on our servers, sell or share any personal information. It has no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no account of its own.

Android (Google Play)

  • The game asks for no device permissions and does not use the internet.
  • Your settings and high scores are saved only on your device, in the game's own app storage. Uninstalling the game deletes them.
  • We do not collect device identifiers, location, contacts, photos or any other data.
  • Google Play may collect information about installs and purchases under Google's own privacy policy.

Windows (Steam)

  • Settings, high scores and optional replay recordings are saved only on your computer.
  • If you play through Steam, your scores are posted to Steam leaderboards together with your Steam account, so other players can see them. This is handled by Valve under the Steam privacy policy. We see only what Steam shows publicly on the leaderboard.

Children

The game collects no personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
